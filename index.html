How it works

Public pages. New roles only.

We check each company’s career page on a five-minute cycle. When a role appears that matches what you asked for — title, seniority, Canada or the US — you get a single email. Nothing from the back catalogue. Nothing you did not ask for.

JobSub is invitation only. You need a subscription key, then a confirmation tap on a link we send you, before any alert goes out.

What it is not

No auto-apply. No resume. No board.

  • We never submit an application for you
  • We do not write resumes or cover letters, and we never ask for one
  • We are not a job board, a recruiter marketplace, or a marketing list
