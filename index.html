What to expect

What JobSub does, and what it never does.

What it does

  • Only brand-new roles Nothing from weeks or months back. Only what just went live.
  • Free, and staying free No fee. JobSub is an independent, not-for-profit research project.
  • Yours to change or leave Re-submit the form to replace your preferences. Every email carries an unsubscribe link that stops sending immediately.

What it never does

  • Take your resume No resume, no phone number, no employment history. An email address and what you are looking for.
  • Apply on your behalf JobSub sends you the posting. It is not a job board, and it never applies for you.
  • Track you No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts on this site. Read the privacy policy.
