JobSub

Get an email within minutes of a job being posted — not days later.

What it does

JobSub monitors the career pages of a curated list of companies. When a company publishes a new role that matches a subscriber's stated preferences, JobSub sends that subscriber a single email — typically within 10 minutes of the posting going live.

It only looks at publicly accessible job listing pages, and it only reports newly published roles.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No auto-apply — JobSub never submits an application on your behalf
  • No resume tailoring or cover-letter generation
  • No resume upload; JobSub never asks for or stores your resume
  • No job board, no recruiter marketplace, no marketing email of any kind

Who can subscribe

JobSub is invitation only and operated by a single person as a personal project. There is no open signup. Subscribing requires a subscription key issued directly by the operator, and every new address must confirm by clicking a link in a confirmation email before any alert is ever sent.

Have a subscription key? Submit or update your preferences here: JobSub preference form. Re-submitting the same form at any time replaces your previous preferences.
Want to stop receiving emails? Every JobSub email contains a one-click unsubscribe link in its footer that requires no login. Unsubscribes take effect immediately. You can also email hello@jobsub.app and your address and preferences will be deleted.

Email practices

  • Addresses are never purchased, scraped, rented, sold, or shared
  • Double opt-in is required before the first alert
  • Every message includes a working one-click unsubscribe link and List-Unsubscribe headers
  • Hard bounces and complaints permanently suppress the address, with no retries
  • Mail is sent from jobsub.app, authenticated with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC

Contact
